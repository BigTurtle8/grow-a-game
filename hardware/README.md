# Hardware

Contained are files used in the process of scoping,
visualizing, and prototyping the Grow a Game.

The vision was a standalone console, but alas,
material and time constraints ran in the way. Regardless,
here are artifacts of our work in progress.

## Contents

 - "Full Console Visualization.png" - A Claude-generated
   visualization of what the console might've looked
   like. Includes the key features of docks for the
   dynamic controllers and arcade-style screen.
 - "Machine Chassis.f3d" - Upon realizing the material
   constraints, the build was drawn back to highlight
   the unique dynamic "controllers" that adapt along
   with the generated game. Created using Autodesk Fusion.
 - "Machine Chassis-{1, 2}.svg" - The SVG files,
   derived from the 3D models, compiled in Inkscape,
   then used to laser cut plywood.
