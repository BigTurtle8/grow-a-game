VERIFIED KIT SPRITES (use these instead of guessing URLs)

Chess (Lichess CBurnett, the pieces people recognize):
  g.chessPiece("wK") g.chessPiece("wQ") g.chessPiece("wR") g.chessPiece("wB") g.chessPiece("wN") g.chessPiece("wP")
  g.chessPiece("bK") g.chessPiece("bQ") g.chessPiece("bR") g.chessPiece("bB") g.chessPiece("bN") g.chessPiece("bP")
  Draw each piece at least 64px on the board. Do not use letter or emoji stand-ins as the main set.

Playing cards (Deck of Cards API):
  g.card("AS") ranks A, 2-9, 0 (ten), J, Q, K plus suits S, H, D, C. g.cardBack().

Pokemon (official sprites):
  g.pokemon(id) front, g.pokemon(id, {back:true}) for the player's battler.
  Common ids: 1 Bulbasaur, 4 Charmander, 7 Squirtle, 25 Pikachu, 16 Pidgey, 19 Rattata,
  133 Eevee, 143 Snorlax, 150 Mewtwo. Items:
  g.image("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokeAPI/sprites/master/sprites/items/poke-ball.png", "o")

Emoji art (Twemoji; good for animals, food, vehicles, weather, trees, dice):
  g.emoji("fish") is wrong — pass the actual emoji character, e.g. g.emoji("🎲")

Other public images:
  g.image(url, fallbackEmoji) — only URLs from the research document or this catalog.
  All URLs are proxied. A bad URL becomes the fallback, which testers treat as a failure,
  so do not guess GitHub paths.

Draw with shapes when that is how the real game looks (Connect Four discs, Pong paddles,
Tetris minos, checker discs, Go stones). Use sprites when the real game has distinct pieces.
